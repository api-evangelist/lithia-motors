When you are choosing your next car, truck, SUV, or minivan, there are probably features that are a must-have for you.  For many car shoppers, all-wheel drive is one of those features.  If you are wondering whether you need this feature in your next ride, it may help to understand some of the advantages it brings.  Lithia Motors Inc is here to tell you about the benefits of this drivetrain. The most popular benefit of this drivetrain is that it gives you better traction and grip, especially on road conditions that are not optimal.  It also provides you with better acceleration because of that superior traction.  Many car shoppers may also like the fact that all-wheel drive leads to a better resale value when you go to sell or trade in your vehicle.
