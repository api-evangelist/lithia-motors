One of the decisions that many car shoppers struggle with when they enter the market for their next car, truck, or SUV is whether they should buy or lease the vehicle.  There are many great reasons to choose either option.  The finance team at a Lithia Motors Inc dealership location can analyze your driving habits and budget to help you decide which option suits your lifestyle the best.  Many people who lease like that they get a new car every few years with the latest features.  They also like that they don’t need to worry about selling their vehicle at the end of the lease. Meanwhile, folks who prefer buying often do so because they don’t need to be concerned with their mileage.  They also like that they will have equity that they can use for their next purchase when their auto loan is paid off.
